# Baked Mac and Cheese, one 13.5 x 9 5/8 x 2 3/4 in pan

Tineke Younger's holiday baked mac and cheese ([TikTok, Dec 2025](https://www.tiktok.com/@tinekeyounger/video/7703704324790111502)), ad-free and rescaled to a single pan measuring **13.5 x 9 5/8 x 2 3/4 in**.

Live page: https://dpsoccerdude101.github.io/quarter-pan-mac-and-cheese/

## The scaling

The video gives no pan size. Its batch — 1 lb dry pasta, 28 fl oz dairy (12 oz evaporated milk + 2 cups cream), 40 oz shredded cheese, 3 tbsp butter + 3 tbsp flour — is about 3½ quarts and is shot in a 9x13.

| Vessel | Footprint | Volume | Batch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9x13 casserole (video) | 117 in² | 4.0 qt at 2 in | 1x |
| Quarter pan, 2½ in | 60 in² | 2.6 qt | ⅔x |
| **This pan** | **130 in²** | **6.2 qt** | **1½x** |

13.5 × 9.625 = 130 in², × 2.75 = 357 in³ = 6.2 qt (5.85 L). That footprint is the half-pan size (12¾ × 10⅜), not a quarter pan.

Pasta and sauce are 1½× the video. With the 48 oz of cheese below, the batch is about 4¼ qt and fills the pan to roughly 2 in, leaving room for the boil and the broil. Doubling would put 7 qt in a 6.2 qt pan.

Cheese is 48 oz here, not the 60 oz a straight 1½× would want: mozzarella 16, medium cheddar 8, sharp cheddar 8, Colby Jack 8, mild cheddar 8. That is a 2:1 cheese-to-pasta ratio rather than the video's 2.5:1 — still a rich baked mac, about 2 in of fill. Each of the five cheeses splits in half: 8 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 per bowl. ("Kobe Jack" read as Colby Jack; Monterey Jack substitutes straight across.)

## Ingredients (1½x)

- 1½ lb dry cavatappi (the video says cavatelli; the pasta on screen is the spiral tube)
- 16 oz low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella, shredded (1 bag)
- 8 oz medium cheddar, block
- 8 oz sharp cheddar, block
- 8 oz Colby Jack, block
- 8 oz mild cheddar, shredded (1 bag)
- 4½ tbsp butter
- 4½ tbsp all-purpose flour
- 18 oz evaporated milk (not condensed)
- 2½ cups heavy cream (trimmed from 1½× so the sauce still bakes set with the tube shape)
- 1½ tbsp Dijon mustard
- 3 tsp kosher salt (1 tbsp Diamond Crystal, 2 tsp Morton's) + pasta water salt
- 3½ tsp smoked paprika
- 2¼ tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp chives

Serves 16 (cut 4 x 4), or 20 smaller scoops.

## Mise en place (build all of this first)

| Container | Holds |
|---|---|
| Ramekin 1 | 3 tsp kosher salt, 3½ tsp smoked paprika, 2¼ tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp pepper, mixed |
| Ramekin 2 | Half of Ramekin 1, for blooming in the butter |
| Pitcher | 18 oz evaporated milk + 2½ cups heavy cream, whisked together |
| Small bowl | 1½ tbsp Dijon |
| Bowl A | Sauce cheese: 8 oz mozzarella, 4 oz medium cheddar, 4 oz sharp cheddar, 4 oz Colby Jack, 4 oz mild cheddar |
| Bowl B | Layering cheese: 8 oz mozzarella, 4 oz medium cheddar, 4 oz sharp cheddar, 4 oz Colby Jack, 4 oz mild cheddar |
| Board | 2 tbsp snipped chives |
| By the stove | 4½ tbsp butter, 4½ tbsp flour |
| Counter | 1½ lb dry cavatappi |

## Method, container by container

1. Pot of water on high, handful of salt. It should taste like the ocean; 1½ lb of pasta is going in.
2. Oven to 350°F, rack middle. Pan out.
3. **Ramekin 2 → pot.** Low heat: melt 4½ tbsp butter, dump in Ramekin 2 (half the seasoning), 30 s, stirring, no browning.
4. **Flour → pot.** 4½ tbsp, 4 to 6 min on low, until it smells toasted, not raw.
5. **Pitcher → pot.** Whisk, a third of the milk-and-cream first, then the rest. Never all at once.
6. **Ramekin 1 → pot, then Dijon.** Rest of the seasoning, then 1½ tbsp mustard. Low, never a boil.
7. **Bowl A → pot.** A handful at a time, each melted before the next.
8. **Cavatappi → pot → pan.** 8 to 9 min (7 if tomorrow), drain, no rinse, fold into sauce. Half in the pan, Bowl B, rest of pasta, rest of Bowl B. Level at 2¼ in.
9. **Pan → oven.** 350°F, 30 to 35 min, broil 2 min. Rest 5 min, chives.
