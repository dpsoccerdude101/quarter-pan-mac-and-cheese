# Baked Mac and Cheese, one 13.5 x 9 5/8 x 2 3/4 in pan

Tineke Younger's holiday baked mac and cheese ([TikTok, Dec 2025](https://www.tiktok.com/@tinekeyounger/video/7703704324790111502)), ad-free and rescaled to a single pan measuring **13.5 x 9 5/8 x 2 3/4 in**.

Live page: https://dpsoccerdude101.github.io/quarter-pan-mac-and-cheese/

## The scaling

The video gives no pan size. Its batch — 1 lb dry pasta, 28 fl oz dairy (12 oz evaporated milk + 2 cups cream), 40 oz shredded cheese, 3 tbsp butter + 3 tbsp flour — is about 3½ quarts and is shot in a 9x13.

| Vessel | Footprint | Volume | Batch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9x13 casserole (video) | 117 in² | 4.0 qt at 2 in | 1x |
| Quarter pan, 2½ in | 60 in² | 2.6 qt | ⅔x |
| **This pan** | **130 in²** | **6.2 qt** | **1½x** |

13.5 × 9.625 = 130 in², × 2.75 = 357 in³ = 6.2 qt (5.85 L). That footprint is the half-pan size (12¾ × 10⅜), not a quarter pan.

Target fill is 2¼ in, about 5 qt, leaving half an inch for the boil and the broil. 1½× the video lands there. Doubling would put 7 qt in a 6.2 qt pan.

## Ingredients (1½x)

- 1½ lb dry cavatappi (the video says cavatelli; the pasta on screen is the spiral tube)
- 1½ lb low-moisture mozzarella, shredded
- 1½ lb Colby Jack, shredded
- 12 oz sharp cheddar, shredded
- 4½ tbsp butter
- 4½ tbsp all-purpose flour
- 18 oz evaporated milk (not condensed)
- 2½ cups heavy cream (trimmed from 1½× so the sauce still bakes set with the tube shape)
- 1½ tbsp Dijon mustard
- 3 tsp kosher salt (1 tbsp Diamond Crystal, 2 tsp Morton's) + pasta water salt
- 3½ tsp smoked paprika
- 2¼ tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp chives

Serves 9 to 12.

## Mise en place (build all of this first)

| Container | Holds |
|---|---|
| Ramekin 1 | 3 tsp kosher salt, 3½ tsp smoked paprika, 2¼ tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp pepper, mixed |
| Ramekin 2 | Half of Ramekin 1, for blooming in the butter |
| Pitcher | 18 oz evaporated milk + 2½ cups heavy cream, whisked together |
| Small bowl | 1½ tbsp Dijon |
| Bowl A | Sauce cheese: 12 oz mozzarella, 12 oz Colby Jack, 6 oz cheddar |
| Bowl B | Layering cheese: the same three, the other halves |
| Board | 2 tbsp snipped chives |
| By the stove | 4½ tbsp butter, 4½ tbsp flour |
| Counter | 1½ lb dry cavatappi |

## Method

1. Boil cavatappi in ocean-salty water, 8 to 9 min (7 if baking tomorrow). Drain, do not rinse.
2. Shred all cheese; split each into two bowls, sauce half and layering half.
3. Mix seasonings; split in half.
4. Low heat: melt butter, bloom half the seasoning 30 s.
5. Add flour, cook 4 to 6 min on low, blond roux.
6. Whisk in evaporated milk slowly, then cream, then the rest of the seasoning. Béchamel, never a boil.
7. Whisk in Dijon, then the sauce-half cheese a handful at a time on low heat.
8. Fold in pasta. Layer: half pasta, half layering cheese, rest of pasta, rest of cheese. Level it at about 2¼ in up the side.
9. 350°F for 30 to 35 min, until the edges bubble and the center is set, then broil 2 min. Rest 5 min, chives.
