# Baked Mac and Cheese, one quarter hotel pan

Tineke Younger's holiday baked mac and cheese ([TikTok, Dec 2025](https://www.tiktok.com/@tinekeyounger/video/7703704324790111502)), ad-free and rescaled to a single quarter hotel pan.

Live page: https://dpsoccerdude101.github.io/quarter-pan-mac/

## The scaling

The video gives no pan size. Its quantities are 1 lb dry pasta, 28 fl oz of dairy (12 oz evaporated milk + 2 cups cream), 40 oz of shredded cheese, 3 tbsp butter + 3 tbsp flour. That is a 9x13x2 casserole, roughly 3 to 3.5 quarts.

| Vessel | Footprint | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| 9x13 casserole (video batch) | 117 in² | ~4.1 qt at 2 in |
| Quarter pan, 2½ in deep | 60 in² | 2.6 qt |
| Quarter pan, 4 in deep | 60 in² | 4.2 qt |

A quarter pan has the same footprint as a 9x13, so depth is the only lever:

- **2½ in deep (standard):** ⅔ of the video batch. Fills it about ¾ and stays under 2½ in, which is what keeps it from going soggy in the middle.
- **4 in deep:** the full video batch, unchanged.

## Ingredients, 2½ in pan (⅔ batch)

- 10 oz dry cavatelli
- 10 oz low-moisture mozzarella, shredded
- 10 oz Colby Jack, shredded
- 5 oz sharp cheddar, shredded
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 8 oz evaporated milk (not condensed)
- 1⅓ cups heavy cream
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1¼ tsp kosher salt, 1½ tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp chives

For the 4 in pan, use 1 lb pasta, 1 lb mozzarella, 1 lb Colby Jack, 8 oz cheddar, 3 tbsp butter, 3 tbsp flour, one 12 oz can evaporated milk, 2 cups cream, 1 tbsp Dijon, 2 tsp salt, 2¼ tsp paprika, 1½ tsp garlic powder, ¾ tsp pepper, 1½ tbsp chives.

## Method

1. Boil pasta in ocean-salty water, 8 min (7 if baking tomorrow). Drain, do not rinse.
2. Shred all cheese; split each into two bowls, sauce half and layering half.
3. Mix seasonings; split in half.
4. Low heat: melt butter, bloom half the seasoning 30 s.
5. Add flour, cook 3 to 5 min, blond roux.
6. Whisk in evaporated milk slowly, then cream, then the rest of the seasoning. Béchamel, never a boil.
7. Whisk in Dijon, then the sauce-half cheese a handful at a time on low heat.
8. Fold in pasta. Layer: half pasta, half layering cheese, rest of pasta, rest of cheese.
9. 350°F for 18 to 22 min (2½ in pan) or 20 to 25 min (4 in pan), then broil 2 min. Rest 5 min, chives.
