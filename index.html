4. Method

  1. Boil the cavatelli in water salted like the ocean, 8 minutes. Baking it tomorrow? 7 minutes. Drain, do not rinse.
  2. Shred every cheese. Split each one into two bowls: half for the sauce, half for layering. Two bowls total, not six.
  3. Small bowl: mix the salt, smoked paprika, garlic powder and pepper. Split that in half too. Half blooms in the butter, half goes into the sauce later.
  4. Low heat: melt the butter, add half the seasoning, bloom 30 seconds. Stir, do not brown.
  5. Add the flour. Cook on low 3 to 5 minutes until it smells like toasted bread, not raw flour.
  6. Switch to a whisk. Stream in the evaporated milk, whisking out every lump, then stream in the heavy cream. Add the other half of the seasoning. Low to medium-low, never a boil.
  7. Whisk in the Dijon, then the sauce-half cheese a handful at a time, letting each melt before the next. Keep the heat low or the cheese breaks and turns grainy. Too thick? Splash of milk.
  8. Fold the pasta into the sauce with a spatula. Half the pasta into the pan, half the layering cheese, remaining pasta, remaining cheese.
  9. 350°F, 18 to 22 minutes, until the edges bubble, then broil 2 minutes for the crust. Rest 5 minutes, scatter the chives, cut and serve.

6. Notes worth reading

  • Pan math. The video never says what dish it uses. 1 lb dry pasta plus 28 oz of dairy and 40 oz of cheese lands around 3 to 3½ quarts, which is a 9×13×2. A quarter pan shares that footprint but is only 2½ in deep, so the small batch is ⅔ of everything and fills it to about ¾. Going deeper than 2½ in is what gets you the soggy middle.
  • Do not substitute condensed milk. Evaporated is unsweetened and reduced; condensed is sweetened and roughly a third sugar.
  • Grainy sauce is heat, not the recipe. Cheese goes in off-boil, low, a handful at a time.
  • Seasoning amounts are eyeballed in the video. Taste the sauce before the pasta goes in and correct it there.
  • Broiler is the last 2 minutes only, and it goes from crust to carbon fast. Stand at the oven.
  • Make-ahead: boil 7 minutes, assemble, refrigerate covered up to 24 hours. Add 8 to 10 minutes to the bake, still covered for the first half.
  • Reheat portions at 350°F covered with foil, splash of milk on top.
